# PJPT (Practical Junior Penetration Tester)

PJPT from **TCM Security** is an affordable, hands-on cert focused on **internal network and Active Directory** attacks. It is a popular first practical cert and a natural lead-in to [PNPT](PNPT.md).

## Exam format

- Hands-on exam against an [Active Directory](../Active%20Directory/Active%20Directory%20Basics.md) network
- You must compromise the domain and reach Domain Admin
- A short report / proof of compromise is expected
- Open-book, generous time window

## What it covers

- Internal network enumeration
- AD attacks: LLMNR poisoning, [Pass-the-Hash](../Active%20Directory/Pass-the-Hash.md), [Kerberoasting](../Active%20Directory/Kerberoasting.md), token impersonation
- Lateral movement and privilege escalation to Domain Admin
- Practical methodology on a realistic domain

## Who it is for

- Beginners who want an AD-focused practical cert cheaply
- A confidence step before PNPT or OSCP
- Career switchers building a portfolio

## How to prepare

- TCM Security's **Practical Ethical Hacking** course
- Build a home AD lab or practice on [GOAD](../Labs/GOAD.md)

## Resources

## Compare

- **PJPT** - internal AD, beginner-friendly, cheap
- **eJPT** - broader beginner practical, no AD focus
- **PNPT** - external + internal + full report
