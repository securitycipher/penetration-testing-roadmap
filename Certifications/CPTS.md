# CPTS (HTB Certified Penetration Testing Specialist)

CPTS from Hack The Box has become a popular, more affordable alternative to OSCP for proving real pentest ability. It is heavier on **Active Directory and chaining** than OSCP and requires a full professional report.

## Exam format

- **10-day** exam window on a realistic enterprise network
- You must compromise the environment and submit a **professional report**
- Pass mark is high (you need to find and report most of the attack path)
- Backed by the HTB Academy **Penetration Tester** job-role path

## What it covers

- Full methodology: recon, enumeration, exploitation, pivoting, post-exploitation
- Deep **Active Directory** attack paths and lateral movement
- Web, network, and service exploitation
- Documentation and client-ready reporting

## Who it is for

- People who want an OSCP-level credential with more AD depth
- Self-learners who like the HTB Academy guided path
- Candidates building a portfolio-grade report

## How to prepare

- Complete the HTB Academy **Penetration Tester** path end to end
- Practice AD-heavy boxes and Pro Labs on [HackTheBox](../Labs/HackTheBox.md)

## Resources

- [HTB CPTS](https://academy.hackthebox.com/preview/certifications/htb-certified-penetration-testing-specialist)

## Compare

- **CPTS** - OSCP-level, more AD and chaining, report required
- **OSCP+** - more brand recognition with recruiters
- **PNPT** - network + AD + report, shorter path
