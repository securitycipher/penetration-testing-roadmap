# PNPT (Practical Network Penetration Tester)

PNPT is TCM Security's intermediate certification focused on **external and internal network pentesting** with a full report deliverable.

## Format

- 5-day exam window
- External pentest on live targets + internal AD-style network
- **Written report required** - tests real-world deliverable skills
- Open-book, open-internet (like real engagements)

## What you learn

- Recon and OSINT
- Active Directory attacks
- Lateral movement and privilege escalation
- Professional report writing

## Who it is for

- People who want a stepping stone before OSCP
- Career switchers who need a portfolio piece (the report)
- Testers who want AD + network focus (OSCP is broader)
