# CC (ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity)

CC is ISC2's **entry-level** cert, aimed at people brand new to security. ISC2 has often offered the exam and training **free** through its "One Million Certified in Cybersecurity" pledge, which makes it a low-risk first credential.

## Exam format

- **100 multiple-choice** questions, 2 hours
- Pass mark 700/1000
- No work experience required (unlike CISSP)

## The five domains

1. Security Principles
2. Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, and Incident Response
3. Access Control Concepts
4. Network Security
5. Security Operations

## Who it is for

- Complete beginners and students
- Career switchers who want a recognized first cert on the resume
- Anyone wanting an on-ramp before Security+ or practical certs

## How to prepare

- ISC2's free self-paced training and practice questions
- Pair with hands-on [Networking](../Networking/OSI%20Model.md) and [Operating System](../Operating%20System/Linux.md) basics

## Resources

- [ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc)

## Compare

- **CC** - free, beginner, theory
- **Security+** - broader, more recognized baseline
- **eJPT** - the practical beginner alternative
