# OSWP (Offensive Security Wireless Professional)

OSWP is OffSec's hands-on **wireless security** cert. It is narrow but practical: you attack real Wi-Fi setups and prove you can break and assess wireless networks. The retired "WiFu" course was replaced by **PEN-210**.

## Course and exam

- Course: **PEN-210** (Foundational Wireless Network Attacks)
- Exam: hands-on, roughly **3 hours 45 minutes** plus report time
- You must attack multiple wireless networks and document the steps

## What it covers

- 802.11 fundamentals, frames, and monitor-mode capture
- Attacking **WEP, WPA/WPA2 PSK** (handshake capture and cracking)
- **WPA Enterprise** and PMKID-based attacks
- Rogue AP / evil-twin basics and client-side attacks
- Using the [Aircrack-ng](../Tools/Aircrack-ng.md) suite and a compatible adapter

## Who it is for

- Testers who do on-site/internal engagements with wireless scope
- Anyone wanting a focused, affordable OffSec practical cert

## How to prepare

- PEN-210 material end to end
- A lab with a supported Wi-Fi adapter (monitor mode + injection)
- Practice capturing and cracking handshakes with [Hashcat](../Tools/Hashcat.md)
