# CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)

CEH from EC-Council is a broad, theory-heavy cert that HR teams and government/DoD roles recognize. The current version is **CEH v13**, which adds a module thread on **AI-driven hacking** and using AI to speed up each attack phase. Hands-on teams respect it less than OSCP, but it clears a lot of job filters.

## Two exams (know the difference)

- **CEH (ANSI)** - 125 multiple-choice questions, 4 hours. This is the "knowledge" exam most people mean.
- **CEH Practical** - 6-hour hands-on exam on a live range (20 real challenges). Pass both and you earn **CEH Master**.

## What it covers

- Recon, scanning, enumeration, and system hacking
- Malware, sniffing, social engineering, denial of service
- Web, wireless, mobile, cloud, and IoT/OT basics
- Cryptography and, in v13, AI-assisted offensive workflows

## Who it is for

- Candidates targeting roles or regions that list CEH by name
- Analysts who want broad coverage before a practical cert
- Compliance-driven environments (it maps to DoD 8140/8570 baselines)

## How to prepare

- Official EC-Council iLabs, plus hands-on practice so CEH Practical is not a shock
- Pair the theory with real labs on [TryHackMe](../Labs/TryHackMe.md)

## Compare

- **CEH** - broad theory + recognition
- **CEH Practical** - adds a hands-on range
- **OSCP+ / CPTS** - far more respected for pure offensive skill
