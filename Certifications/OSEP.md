# OSEP (Offensive Security Experienced Penetration Tester)

OSEP is OffSec's advanced cert for **evasion and lateral movement** against defended environments. It is the natural follow-up to OSCP for people heading toward red teaming, with a strong focus on getting past antivirus and EDR.

## Course and exam

- Course: **PEN-300** (Evasion Techniques and Breaching Defenses)
- Exam: **47 hours 45 minutes** hands-on, then 24 hours to report
- Part of the OSCE3 cert set (with OSWE and OSED)

## What it covers

- Bypassing **antivirus and application whitelisting** (AppLocker, WDAC)
- Custom payloads and tradecraft in C#, PowerShell, and VBA
- Phishing and client-side initial access
- Advanced [Active Directory](../Active%20Directory/Active%20Directory%20Basics.md) attacks and lateral movement
- Pivoting and bypassing network restrictions

## Prerequisites

- OSCP-level skills strongly recommended
- Comfortable scripting and reading Windows internals

## How to prepare

- PEN-300 labs end to end (they are large - budget time)
- Build EDR-evasion intuition; understand what detections you are defeating
