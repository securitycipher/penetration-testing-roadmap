# CRTP (Certified Red Team Professional)

CRTP from Pentester Academy focuses on **Active Directory attacks** in a lab environment. It is the standard follow-up after OSCP for internal/red team work.

## What it covers

- AD enumeration with PowerView and BloodHound
- Kerberoasting, AS-REP roasting, delegation abuse
- Lateral movement (PSRemoting, WMI, DCOM)
- Domain persistence (Golden Ticket, Silver Ticket, DCSync)
- AV/EDR evasion basics

## Prerequisites

- Comfortable with Windows and Linux
- OSCP or equivalent hands-on experience recommended
- Basic AD knowledge helps but course teaches from ground up

## Exam format

- 24-hour practical lab exam
- Compromise the domain (get Domain Admin proof)
- Real AD lab, not CTF-style puzzles
