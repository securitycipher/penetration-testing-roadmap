# CRTE (Certified Red Team Expert)

CRTE from **Altered Security** is the advanced follow-up to [CRTP](CRTP.md). It covers a larger, multi-forest **Active Directory** environment and tougher attack paths, bridging the gap toward full red-team operations.

## What it covers

- Advanced AD enumeration across multiple domains and forests
- **Cross-forest and cross-trust** attacks
- Advanced Kerberos abuse (constrained/unconstrained/RBCD delegation)
- Forest persistence and trust-key attacks
- Evading basic defenses while moving laterally

## Prerequisites

- CRTP-level AD knowledge strongly recommended
- Comfort with PowerShell, [BloodHound](../Active%20Directory/BloodHound.md), and Kerberos concepts

## Exam format

- **48-hour** hands-on lab exam, then 48 hours to submit the report
- Compromise multiple domains/forests and document the full path

## How to prepare

- Altered Security's advanced AD course labs
- Drill delegation and trust attacks in [GOAD](../Labs/GOAD.md)

## Resources

- [Altered Security CRTE](https://www.alteredsecurity.com/adlab)

## Compare

- **CRTP** - single-domain AD fundamentals
- **CRTE** - multi-forest, advanced AD
- **CRTO** - full red-team ops with C2 and evasion
