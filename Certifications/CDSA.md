# CDSA (HTB Certified Defensive Security Analyst)

CDSA from Hack The Box is a hands-on **blue-team / SOC analyst** cert. Pentesters benefit from it the same way they benefit from [BTL1](BTL1.md): understanding detection makes you a better, stealthier attacker and opens purple-team roles.

## Exam format

- Multi-day, **scenario-based** practical exam
- You investigate a simulated intrusion, analyze logs and telemetry, and write an **incident report**
- Backed by the HTB Academy **SOC Analyst** job-role path

## What it covers

- Security monitoring and [SIEM](../Terminology/SIEM.md) analysis (Splunk, ELK)
- Detection engineering and alert triage
- Log analysis across endpoints and network
- Threat intelligence and the MITRE ATT&CK framework
- Incident response process and reporting

## Who it is for

- Aspiring SOC analysts and detection engineers
- Pentesters who want defensive awareness (purple team)
- Anyone who wants the hiring-rich blue-team side (see the FAQ on red vs blue)

## How to prepare

- Complete the HTB Academy **SOC Analyst** path
- Practice on [CyberDefenders](../Labs/TryHackMe.md) and blue-team rooms
