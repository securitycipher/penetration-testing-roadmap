# Proving Grounds (OffSec)

Proving Grounds (PG) is OffSec's hands-on lab platform of vulnerable machines. It is the closest practice you can get to the [OSCP](../Certifications/OSCP.md) exam style, because the boxes are built and curated by OffSec themselves. Most people grinding toward OSCP+ spend serious time here.

## The two tiers

- **PG Play** - free machines (a curated set of community/VulnHub boxes) hosted by OffSec. Good starting point, no cost.
- **PG Practice** - paid subscription; OffSec-authored boxes with official walkthroughs, difficulty ratings, and a style that mirrors the exam.

## Why it is good for OSCP prep

- Boxes feel like the exam: realistic, no rabbit-hole CTF tricks
- Mix of Linux and Windows, plus [Active Directory](../Active%20Directory/Active%20Directory%20Basics.md) practice
- Community-maintained difficulty lists (for example the "PG Practice like OSCP" lists) tell you which boxes to prioritize
- Official write-ups let you learn when truly stuck

## How to use it

1. Enumerate thoroughly first - PG rewards methodology, not guessing (see [Pentest Phases](../Methodology/Pentest%20Phases.md)).
2. Work a curated OSCP-like list rather than random boxes.
3. Practice full write-ups for each box to build the reporting habit the exam needs.
4. Time-box yourself to simulate exam pressure.

## How it compares

- **[HackTheBox](HackTheBox.md)** - huge variety, strong AD/Pro Labs
- **[TryHackMe](TryHackMe.md)** - more guided, beginner-friendly
- **Proving Grounds** - closest to the OSCP exam feel
